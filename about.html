About Book Explorer

A simple way to find books and keep the ones you love.

What this app does

Book Explorer lets you search for books by title, see their covers, authors and publication years, and save your favorites. Your favorites stay on your device, so they are still there when you come back.

Features

Browse books

Real book data loaded from the Open Library API.

Search by title

Type a title and press Search or Enter.

Save favorites

Add or remove books and keep them after a refresh.

Works on any screen

The layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops.

Built with

  • HTML
  • Tailwind CSS
  • JavaScript (ES6 modules)
  • fetch and async/await
  • localStorage
  • Git and GitHub
